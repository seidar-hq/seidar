Seidar includes AI-powered analysis for individual assets and for your overall portfolio. The reports are designed to help you interpret Stellar market data, protocol activity, and your own positioning — not to replace your judgement.

## AI token insights

Open any asset from the Dashboard and click the **sparkle** icon in the **AI Insights** column to generate an analysis.

Each token report covers:

| Section | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **Price Trend** | Price action, momentum, volume, and relevant technical indicators |
| **Market & On-Chain Signals** | Stellar DEX liquidity and pool depth, large wallet flows, protocol activity, and market positioning |
| **Protocol Context** | Where relevant, lending utilisation, vault yields, or perps funding and open interest |
| **Risks & Resistance** | Liquidity risk, issuer or counterparty risk, volatility, contract risk, and key resistance levels |
| **Final Outlook** | An overall bias — **Bullish**, **Neutral**, or **Bearish** |

The analysis uses publicly available market and on-chain data. It may flag assets with thin liquidity, unusual activity, or other risk signals, but it is not a security audit.

## AI portfolio report

Click **AI Report** at the top of the Dashboard's Assets tab to generate a report for your portfolio.

The report contains:

| Section | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **Portfolio Summary** | Your composition, major exposures, and overall risk posture |
| **Portfolio Allocation** | Per-asset current allocation, suggested allocation, bias, key factor, and a suggested action (accumulate, hold, or reduce) |
| **Portfolio Analysis** | Concentration risk, diversification, rebalancing considerations, and relevant macro or ecosystem factors |

You can generate a report for your full portfolio or for the current wallet and protocol filters.

## Risk level customisation

Both AI features support three risk profiles:

| Risk level | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| **Conservative** | Capital preservation, downside risk, and stable allocations |
| **Balanced** | A balance between upside potential and downside risk |
| **Aggressive** | Upside potential, momentum, and growth opportunities |

Token insights use your selected profile automatically. In the portfolio report, you can switch profiles in the report dialog; each change generates a fresh analysis.

## Privacy and limitations

- **No wallet addresses are shared with AI providers** — only asset names, symbols, and publicly available market and protocol data.
- Analysis is based on public data that may be incomplete, delayed, or wrong.
- AI-generated content is **not financial advice**.
- Reports can be unavailable when the AI provider is unavailable.

:::caution
AI Insights are automated analyses generated by AI models. Use them as one input among many, not as the sole basis for a trading or investment decision.
:::
